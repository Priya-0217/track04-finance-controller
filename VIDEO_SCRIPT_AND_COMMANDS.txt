MINUTE 3:45 - 4:45 | WHAT BROKE AND HOW WE GOT OUT (THE WINNING FILTER)

"Now for the part Razorpay values most: What broke, and how we got out. 

We documented 8 real engineering failures in WHAT_BROKE.md, but three were 
critical:

First, The 5-Token Thinking Starvation. Early on, our Gemini Flash responses 
kept terminating after just 10 words. When we inspected raw HTTP usage metadata, 
we discovered that Gemini's internal reasoning tokens -- thoughtsTokenCount -- 
were burning 238 tokens out of our 250-token budget! We solved this by boosting 
maxOutputTokens to 2048 across all streaming routers.

Second, Cascade Preemption. In early runs, Tier 1 and Tier 3 dropped to zero 
because Tier 2 had too broad a tolerance window -- it greedily swallowed exact 
matches before Tier 3 ever evaluated the text. We fixed this by constraining 
Tier 2 strictly to true fee deltas and adding regex ID extraction in Tier 1.

Third, The International Card MDR Floor. Four card transactions kept failing 
in the 106-record dataset. We realized Indian international cards incur 3.5% 
base MDR plus flat 7 Rupees gateway fee plus 18% GST, pushing total deductions 
to ~4.4%. We updated our tolerance ceiling to 4.80% and sanitized merchant string 
whitespace, pushing our match rate to 83.96% with zero false positives."
